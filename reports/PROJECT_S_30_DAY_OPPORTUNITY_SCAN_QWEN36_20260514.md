## 2) Project-S Current Capability Fit
Project-S’s architecture aligns precisely with the identified opportunity:
- **Persistent self_state + multi-AI coordination:** Enables long-running, stateful agentic workflows that can hand off tasks across specialized models without losing context.
- **Witness evidence trail:** Directly satisfies 2026’s market requirement for AI audit trails, compliance logging, and human-in-the-loop verification.
- **Web search + scrape + synthesis:** Provides the core data ingestion and research engine needed for market intelligence, competitive analysis, and automated reporting.
- **Local/server ambitions:** Positions Project-S to serve privacy-conscious small teams and one-person businesses that cannot or will not route sensitive workflows through third-party cloud APIs.
- **Multi-tool orchestration:** Allows cross-platform automation (e.g., CRM updates, content drafting, data aggregation) without custom integrations.
