## Confirmed Facts
- Mathematics originated from practical human activities including counting, measuring, and describing object shapes.
- Early written mathematical developments emerged around 3000 BC in Mesopotamian states (Sumer, Akkad, Assyria) and Ancient Egypt.
- The Greek approach to logic and formal proofs established the cornerstone for modern mathematical methodology.
- Between the 8th and 14th centuries, Islamic scholars translated Greek and Indian texts, leading to the creation of algebra and refined number systems.
- Mathematical notation is critical for the easy use, understanding, and propagation of mathematical concepts.
- Modern mathematics has evolved into complex algorithms that power contemporary technologies, including artificial intelligence.
- Frank Ramsey made significant contributions to mathematics, logic, and economics before his death at age 26.
- Bernhard Riemann and Kurt Gödel represent key figures in the transition to modern mathematical thought.
- Morris Kline’s *Mathematical Thought From Ancient To Modern Times* is recognized as a definitive historical reference tracing developments from Babylonia and Egypt to modern theorists.
